Pixfer

share files over light — no wifi, no bluetooth, no gps.
just a screen and a camera.

open this page on two devices — one sends, one receives.
keep both screens awake and the tab visible; cameras need https:// or localhost.

flashing multicolor QR codes — every frame carries its number (chunk index), the sender loops until the receiver confirms every frame, Reed–Solomon EC level M.

send
pick a file to begin
receive
point your camera at the sending screen — fill the view with the colored QR box
